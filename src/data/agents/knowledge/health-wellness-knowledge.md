# Health & Wellness Knowledge Base - Dr. Priya Sharma

## Core Expertise Areas

### 1. Stress Management
- **Physiological Response**: Understanding fight-or-flight, cortisol effects, chronic stress impact
- **Techniques**: Progressive muscle relaxation, box breathing, 4-7-8 breathing, body scan meditation
- **Lifestyle Factors**: Sleep hygiene, exercise, nutrition, social connection
- **Warning Signs**: When stress becomes burnout or anxiety disorder

### 2. Sleep Optimization
- **Sleep Hygiene Principles**:
  - Consistent sleep-wake schedule (even weekends)
  - Dark, cool room (18-20°C ideal)
  - No screens 1 hour before bed
  - Avoid caffeine after 2 PM
  - Wind-down routine (15-30 mins)
- **Common Issues**: Insomnia, sleep apnea symptoms, restless sleep
- **Natural Aids**: Chamomile tea, magnesium, lavender, warm milk with turmeric

### 3. Nutrition & Energy
- **Balanced Eating**: Importance of protein, complex carbs, healthy fats
- **Indian Diet Optimization**: Daal-roti-sabzi balance, reducing refined carbs
- **Energy Management**: Blood sugar stability, meal timing, hydration
- **Mindful Eating**: Slow eating, hunger vs. emotional eating

### 4. Mental Wellness
- **Mindfulness Practices**: Daily meditation (start with 5 mins), gratitude journaling
- **Cognitive Patterns**: Recognizing negative thought spirals
- **Self-Compassion**: Treating oneself with kindness
- **Digital Wellness**: Screen time management, social media breaks

### 5. Physical Activity
- **Types**: Cardio, strength, flexibility, balance
- **Indian Practices**: Yoga asanas, pranayama, walking
- **Starting Small**: 10-minute walks, desk stretches
- **Consistency Over Intensity**: Regular gentle movement beats occasional intense workouts

### 6. Holistic Indian Wellness Practices
- **Ayurvedic Principles**: Basic dosha concepts, seasonal eating
- **Yoga Therapy**: Specific asanas for stress, sleep, energy
- **Pranayama**: Anulom Vilom, Kapalbhati, Bhramari
- **Traditional Remedies**: Turmeric milk, tulsi tea, ashwagandha basics

## Common Conditions & Approaches

### Chronic Fatigue
- Rule out: Thyroid, anemia, vitamin D deficiency
- Address: Sleep, stress, nutrition, movement
- Recommend: Complete health checkup, gradual lifestyle changes

### Anxiety Symptoms
- Validate: It's real and common
- Teach: Grounding techniques (5-4-3-2-1 sensory), breathing
- Explore: Triggers, thought patterns
- Refer: If persistent or severe (to mental health professional)

### Weight Concerns
- Approach: Non-judgmental, focus on health not appearance
- Principles: Sustainable changes, no crash diets
- Address: Emotional eating, stress eating
- Encourage: Body acceptance, movement for joy

### Headaches & Body Pain
- Explore: Hydration, posture, stress, sleep
- Suggest: Stretching, ergonomics, relaxation
- Refer: If persistent or severe

## Red Flags - When to Refer

- Suicidal thoughts or severe depression
- Eating disorder symptoms
- Substance abuse
- Severe, persistent physical symptoms
- Signs of serious medical conditions
- Abuse or trauma requiring specialized support
