# Financial Advice Knowledge Base - Rahul Kapoor

## Core Expertise Areas

### 1. Budgeting Fundamentals
- **50-30-20 Rule**:
  - 50% Needs (rent, utilities, groceries, EMIs)
  - 30% Wants (entertainment, dining, shopping)
  - 20% Savings & Debt Repayment
- **Zero-Based Budgeting**: Every rupee has a purpose
- **Envelope Method**: Physical or digital allocation
- **Tracking Tools**: Apps (Money Manager, Walnut), spreadsheets, notebooks

### 2. Emergency Fund
- **Target**: 3-6 months of expenses (essential expenses only)
- **Where to Keep**: Savings account, liquid funds, FD
- **Priority**: Build before investing
- **Usage**: Job loss, medical emergency, urgent repairs only

### 3. Debt Management
- **Types**: Credit cards, personal loans, home loans, education loans
- **Good vs. Bad Debt**: Home loan (appreciating asset) vs. credit card debt
- **Payoff Strategies**:
  - Avalanche Method: Highest interest rate first
  - Snowball Method: Smallest debt first (psychological wins)
- **Credit Score**: Importance, how to check, how to improve

### 4. Saving Strategies
- **Pay Yourself First**: Auto-debit to savings on salary day
- **Separate Accounts**: Goals-based (emergency, travel, big purchase)
- **Where to Save**:
  - Savings account (low return, high liquidity)
  - Fixed Deposits (higher return, less liquidity)
  - Recurring Deposits (disciplined monthly saving)
  - Liquid Funds (better than savings account)

### 5. Investment Basics
- **Risk & Return**: Higher risk = potential for higher return
- **Investment Vehicles (India)**:
  - Mutual Funds (equity, debt, hybrid)
  - PPF (Public Provident Fund) - tax-free, 15-year lock-in
  - NPS (National Pension Scheme) - retirement
  - FDs (Fixed Deposits) - safe, low return
  - Stocks - direct equity, high risk
  - Gold - physical, digital, ETFs
  - Real Estate - high capital, illiquid
- **SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)**: Power of rupee-cost averaging
- **Diversification**: Don't put all eggs in one basket
- **Asset Allocation**: Based on age, goals, risk tolerance

### 6. Tax Planning (India)
- **Section 80C**: ₹1.5 lakh (PPF, ELSS, LIC, EPF, NSC)
- **Section 80D**: Health insurance premiums
- **HRA Exemptions**: For salaried individuals
- **Tax Regime Choice**: Old vs. New (post 2020)
- **When to Consult CA**: Complex situations, filing help

## Common Financial Situations

### Struggling with Debt
- **First Step**: List all debts with interest rates and minimum payments
- **Strategy**: Pay minimums on all, attack one aggressively
- **Avoid**: Taking new debt to pay old debt (unless consolidating at lower rate)
- **Mental Health**: Acknowledge stress, small wins matter
- **Severe Cases**: Debt counseling services

### Can't Save Anything
- **Audit Expenses**: Track for 1 month, find leaks
- **Start Tiny**: Even ₹500/month matters
- **Automate**: Remove decision fatigue
- **Increase Income**: Side hustles, upskilling

### Beginner Investor
- **Step 1**: Emergency fund first
- **Step 2**: Clear high-interest debt
- **Step 3**: Start SIP in index fund or balanced fund
- **Step 4**: Learn, diversify, increase over time
- **Avoid**: Tips, get-rich-quick schemes, timing market

### Financial Stress
- **Validate**: Money stress is real and common
- **Control What You Can**: Budget, small goals
- **Perspective**: Situation is temporary, solvable
- **Action**: One step today, not everything at once

### Big Purchase Planning
- **Define**: What, why, when, how much
- **Save**: Dedicated fund, timeline
- **Avoid**: Lifestyle inflation, impulse
- **Loan Decision**: Interest rate, impact on budget

## Indian Financial Products & Terms

### Banking
- **Savings Account**: Basic, zero-balance, salary accounts
- **Fixed Deposit**: Interest rates, premature withdrawal
- **Recurring Deposit**: Monthly fixed saving

### Investment
- **Mutual Funds**: NFO, NAV, exit load, expense ratio
- **PPF**: 15-year, government-backed, tax-free
- **NPS**: Tier 1 & 2, annuity requirement
- **ELSS**: 3-year lock-in, equity, Section 80C

### Insurance
- **Term Insurance**: Pure protection, most important
- **Health Insurance**: Family floater, critical illness
- **Avoid**: ULIPs, endowment (as investment)

### Loans
- **Home Loan**: Pre-approval, interest types, tenure
- **Personal Loan**: High interest, use cautiously
- **Education Loan**: Moratorium, interest subsidy
- **Gold Loan**: Lower interest, risk of losing gold

## Key Financial Principles

### Compound Interest
- "The 8th wonder of the world"
- Start early, even small amounts grow
- ₹5000/month for 20 years at 12% = ₹50 lakhs+

### Inflation
- Money loses value over time
- Savings account doesn't beat inflation
- Invest to grow wealth

### Lifestyle Inflation
- As income grows, expenses often grow too
- Maintain lifestyle, invest raises
- "Live like a student a little longer"

### Behavior > Strategy
- Best plan fails without discipline
- Automate to remove emotion
- Small consistent steps beat big sporadic efforts

## Key Phrases & Responses

### Opening Questions
- "What's your biggest worry about money right now?"
- "How would you describe your relationship with money?"
- "What does financial security mean to you?"

### Validation Statements
- "Money worries are incredibly common - you're not alone"
- "Nobody taught us this in school, so it's okay to not know"
- "Every rupee saved today is a gift to future you"

### Empowerment Phrases
- "You can absolutely turn this around"
- "Financial wellness is built one decision at a time"
- "It's not about what you make, it's about what you keep"

### Action Prompts
- "What's one expense you could reduce this week?"
- "Can we set up auto-transfer to savings today?"
- "Let's create a simple plan you can actually follow"

## Red Flags - When to Refer

- Severe debt requiring legal/professional help
- Addiction (gambling, shopping) requiring therapy
- Depression/anxiety requiring mental health support
- Complex tax situations requiring CA
- Investment fraud requiring legal action
- Domestic financial abuse
