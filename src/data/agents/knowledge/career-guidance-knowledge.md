## Common Career Challenges & Approaches

### Feeling Stuck/Plateaued
- Explore: What growth means to them
- Identify: Internal opportunities, skill gaps
- Consider: Lateral moves, new projects, visibility strategies
- Plan: 6-12 month growth roadmap

### Career Confusion (Multiple Interests)
- Technique: Eliminate by experimentation, not just thinking
- Approach: Informational interviews, side projects
- Perspective: Career is long - you can explore multiple paths
- Action: Choose one to start, commit to learning

### Imposter Syndrome
- Validate: Extremely common, especially in high achievers
- Reframe: Focus on growth, not perfection
- Evidence: Document achievements and positive feedback
- Action: Share fears with trusted people

### Work-Life Balance Struggles
- Assess: Boundaries, priorities, energy management
- Discuss: What balance means to them specifically
- Strategies: Time blocking, saying no, delegation
- Perspective: Career marathon, not sprint

### Salary Dissatisfaction
- Research: Market rates (Glassdoor, networking)
- Prepare: Document value and achievements
- Negotiate: Timing, approach, total compensation view
- Consider: When to stay and fight vs. move on

## Key Frameworks & Tools

### Career Decision Matrix
- List options
- Define criteria (salary, growth, purpose, location, etc.)
- Weight criteria by importance
- Score options, calculate weighted scores

### SWOT Analysis for Career
- Strengths: What you excel at
- Weaknesses: Gaps to address
- Opportunities: Market trends, connections
- Threats: Automation, competition

### Ikigai Exploration
- What you love
- What you're good at
- What the world needs
- What you can be paid for

## Key Phrases & Responses

### Discovery Questions
- "What does success look like for you in 5 years?"
- "When do you feel most alive at work?"
- "What would you do if money wasn't a constraint?"

### Reframe Statements
- "Every experience has prepared you for something"
- "Your unique combination of skills is your superpower"
- "There's no wrong path, only information"

### Action Prompts
- "What's one thing you could do this week to explore this?"
- "Who in your network is doing something similar?"
- "What skill, if you built it, would change everything?"

## Red Flags - When to Refer

- Severe anxiety or depression affecting function
- Burnout requiring mental health support
- Workplace harassment or legal issues
- Financial crisis requiring financial counselor
