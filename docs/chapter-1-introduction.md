# CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION

## 1.1 Background of the Study

Approximately one in four college students experiences significant mental health issues, yet only 20% seek professional support (WHO, 2024). Traditional counseling services face severe limitations: fixed operating hours, high counselor-to-student ratios, long wait times (2-3 weeks average), and stigma barriers. MindfulU addresses these challenges through AI-powered conversational agents combined with realistic avatar technology, providing 24/7 accessible counseling support through platforms like LiveKit for real-time communication and Beyond Presence for avatar rendering.

## 1.2 Problem Statement

Current challenges in student counseling include: (1) Limited accessibility with fixed hours and no crisis support, (2) Overwhelming demand causing 2-3 week wait times, (3) Stigma preventing students from seeking help, (4) High costs ($100-$200/hour) creating financial barriers, and (5) Lack of specialized counselors for career, academic, and financial guidance.

## 1.3 Proposed Solution

MindfulU provides 24/7 accessible counseling through: **Real-time Video Sessions** using LiveKit and Beyond Presence for lifelike avatar interactions; **Voice Call Scheduling** via n8n automation; **Six Specialized Counselors** - Dr. Priya Sharma (Health & Wellness), Arjun Mehta (Career Guidance), Dr. Anjali Reddy (Academic Support), Rahul Kapoor (Financial Advice), Neha Patel (Social & Relationships), and Rohan Gupta (Personal Development); **Multi-Language Support** for English, Hindi, and Hinglish; and **Firebase Authentication** ensuring secure, private access.

## 1.4 Objectives

**Primary**: (1) Develop AI-powered counseling platform with video communication, (2) Integrate LiveKit and Beyond Presence for real-time avatar sessions, (3) Create six specialized counselor personas with domain expertise.

**Secondary**: (1) Implement Firebase authentication, (2) Build responsive React/TypeScript interface, (3) Ensure 24/7 availability, (4) Support multi-language communication, (5) Provide session management and history tracking.

## 1.5 Scope

**In Scope**: Web-based responsive application, real-time video sessions with AI avatars, voice call scheduling, six counseling domains, Firebase authentication, onboarding/dashboard, session management.

**Out of Scope**: Native mobile apps, institutional database integration, payment processing, offline functionality, crisis intervention services.

## 1.6 Significance

This project advances AI counseling by demonstrating practical integration of LiveKit, Beyond Presence, and Firebase; providing specialized multi-domain support beyond generic chatbots; enhancing accessibility through 24/7 availability and low cost; reducing stigma via private interactions; enabling scalability to serve unlimited concurrent users; and implementing culturally adapted multi-language support.

## 1.7 Technology Stack

**Frontend**: React 18, TypeScript, Vite, shadcn/ui, Radix UI, Tailwind CSS  
**Backend Services**: Firebase Authentication, LiveKit (WebRTC), Beyond Presence (Avatars), n8n (Workflows)  
**State & Routing**: React Query, React Router v6, React Context
